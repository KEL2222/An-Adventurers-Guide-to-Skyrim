Culture

Religion

Most people in Skyrim worship the Nine Divines, the Empire's gods. Temples dedicated to these deities can be found throughout Skyrim, offering blessings, healing, and guidance to travellers.

The Thu'um

The Voice, known as the Thu'um, is the ancient language of dragons. Through intense discipline, a speaker can channel powerful Words of Power capable of reshaping the battlefield or influencing the world itself.

The Dragon Cult

Long before Skyrim became the province it is today, the Dragon Cult ruled over the land. Dragon Priests served their dragon masters, and their ancient tombs can still be found scattered throughout Skyrim.

The Nine Holds

Skyrim is divided into nine Holds, each ruled by a Jarl. Every Hold possesses its own traditions, architecture, economy, and culture while remaining united under the province of Skyrim.
