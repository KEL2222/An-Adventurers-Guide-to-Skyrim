Food

Traditional Cuisine

Skyrim's cuisine is simple, hearty and designed to endure the province's harsh climate. Meals commonly consist of soups, stews, roasted meats, fresh bread and locally grown vegetables. Inns throughout the province provide hot meals for weary travellers, while wealthier citizens enjoy sweet pastries and elaborate feasts.

Whiterun, Riften and the fertile Rift produce many of Skyrim's crops, supplying much of the province with vegetables, wheat and livestock.

Harvesting Food

Travellers looking to earn a little extra coin can help local farmers harvest crops throughout Skyrim. Wheat, potatoes, cabbages, leeks and gourds are grown across the province, particularly around Whiterun where fertile land supports numerous farms.

Farming offers a peaceful alternative to adventuring and provides fresh ingredients for cooking.

Traveller's Advice

Before setting out into Skyrim's wilderness, ensure you are properly prepared. The province's climate is unforgiving and dangerous wildlife is never far away. A well-stocked backpack can make the difference between life and death.

Always Carry

  • Health, Magicka and Stamina potions.
  • Cooked meals to restore health and stamina.
  • Arrows suitable for your chosen bow.
  • A reliable weapon and repaired armour.
  • Ingredients for emergency alchemy.
  • Lockpicks and a torch when exploring ruins.
