The Orcish Invasion

Decades ago, an army of orcs invaded a sanctuary in Dawnstar, Nightcaller Temple. A temple of Daedric Prince Vaermina, lady of nightmares. The remnants still remain.

Headless Horseman

There are many beliefs surrounding the Headless Rider. Some Nords believe it is Ragnar the Red, the protagonist sparing the same name in the popular song sung by bards. Others believe it is Hamvir, as the headless horseman will lead you to Hamvir's Rest on its nightly walk there.

Ysgramor

Ysgramor, the patron of the Companions, based in Whiterun, has the most violent history in Skyrim. The Snow Elves attacked the human settlers in early Skyrim due to the rapid growth in population and expansion in the city of Saarthal.

Ysgramor and his sons managed to flee back to Atmora. He returned with the Five Hundred Companions to reclaim Skyrim, driving the Snow Elves from many of their strongholds and changing the history of the province forever.
