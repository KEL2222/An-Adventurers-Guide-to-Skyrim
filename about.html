About Skyrim

Skyrim is the northernmost province of Tamriel, renowned for its towering mountain ranges, icy tundra's, dense forests, and rugged coastline. Home to the proud and resilient Nords, the province is a land shaped by harsh winters, ancient traditions, and a rich history stretching back thousands of years.

The province is divided into nine Holds, each governed by a Jarl and featuring its own distinctive landscape, culture, and capital city. From the fertile plains surrounding Whiterun to the snow-covered peaks of the Pale and the volcanic hot springs of Eastmarch, Skyrim offers an extraordinary variety of natural environments.

Skyrim is also famous for its ancient Nordic ruins, towering fortresses, and legendary landmarks such as the Throat of the World, the highest mountain in Tamriel. Dragons, powerful magic, and centuries-old legends are woven into the province's identity, making it a place where history and myth exist side by side.

Whether visitors come to explore breathtaking wilderness, discover forgotten ruins, experience traditional Nordic culture, or seek adventure across its untamed landscapes, Skyrim offers an unforgettable journey through one of Tamriel's most iconic provinces.
