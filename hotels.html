No journey across Skyrim is complete without staying at one of its welcoming inns. Found in every major city and many smaller settlements, inns provide warm beds, hearty meals, and a chance to rest before continuing your adventures.

If the journey gets lonely, mercenaries can be hired in many of Skyrim's cities. Warriors, mages and archers are all available to accompany travellers for a fee of 500 gold.

Recommended Inns

  • The Winking Skeever (Solitude)
  • The Bannered Mare (Whiterun)
  • The Bee and Barb (Riften)
  • The Sleeping Giant Inn (Riverwood)
  • Nightgate Inn (Eastmarch)

Transport

Carriages travel between every major city and cost either 20 or 50 gold depending on distance.

Ferries operate from Dawnstar, Solitude and Windhelm for 50 gold.
