What To Do In Skyrim

College of Winterhold

If you are ready to be educated in the ways of magic, the College of Winterhold sits on the edge of Winterhold and contains masters in all five schools of magic.

The Skyforge

The Skyforge overlooking Whiterun is one of the most famous landmarks in Skyrim. The origins of the forge remain unknown and visitors are welcome to witness Eorlund Gray-Mane crafting legendary weapons.

Blackreach

Hidden beneath Skyrim lies Blackreach, a vast underground cavern filled with glowing fungi, ancient Dwemer ruins, and unique wildlife unlike anywhere else in Tamriel.

Shrine of Azura

Standing high in the mountains east of Winterhold, the Shrine of Azura honours the Daedric Prince of dusk and dawn and offers spectacular panoramic views.

Understone Keep

Beneath Markarth lies Understone Keep, home to one of Skyrim's finest collections of Dwemer artefacts and the renowned court wizard Calcelmo.

Dawnstar Museum

Those fascinated by the mysterious should visit the Museum of the Mythic Dawn in Dawnstar, where relics connected to Mehrunes Dagon can be examined.

High Hrothgar

Climb the Seven Thousand Steps to High Hrothgar, home of the Greybeards. Along the path you will discover engraved tablets describing the history of the Voice.

Labyrinthian

Explore one of Skyrim's largest Nordic ruins. Labyrinthian contains Dragon Priest tombs, ancient puzzles and the remains of one of Tamriel's oldest cities.
