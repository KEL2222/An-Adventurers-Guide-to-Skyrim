Commerce Across Skyrim

Every Hold throughout Skyrim supports a thriving economy. From the bustling streets of Solitude to the market squares of Whiterun, merchants provide travellers with food, equipment, clothing, crafting materials and magical supplies. Nearly every settlement has a general goods store alongside specialist traders such as blacksmiths, apothecaries and court wizards.

Adventurers are encouraged to sell treasures recovered from caves, Nordic ruins and Dwemer cities. Merchants will buy most equipment, helping travellers fund their next journey.

Merchant Services

Travellers can expect to find a wide variety of services throughout Skyrim including:

  • Blacksmiths repairing and selling weapons and armour.
  • Alchemists providing ingredients, potions and poisons.
  • General goods merchants purchasing unwanted loot.
  • Food vendors supplying cooked meals and fresh produce.
  • Court Wizards selling spell tomes and magical equipment.
  • Jewellers trading gems, jewellery and precious metals.

Merchants Who Offer Training

Several merchants and craftsmen are recognised masters in their professions and are willing to train aspiring adventurers for a fee.

  • Arcadia – Alchemy (Whiterun)
  • Balimund – Smithing (Riften)
  • Revyn Sadri – Speech (Windhelm)
  • Sergius Turrianus – Enchanting (College of Winterhold)
  • Colette Marence – Restoration (College of Winterhold)
  • Giraud Gemane – Speech (Bards College, Solitude)

Improving these skills will help adventurers negotiate better prices, create stronger equipment and survive Skyrim's harsh wilderness.

Trade and Economy

Each Hold has developed its own economy around local resources. Whiterun exports grain and livestock, Markarth mines silver, Windhelm thrives on fishing and trade, Riften supports agriculture and timber production, while Solitude serves as Skyrim's principal port.

Caravans and travelling merchants regularly move between settlements, ensuring goods are distributed across the province. Prices are influenced by a traveller's Speech skill and reputation, rewarding experienced traders with better deals.
